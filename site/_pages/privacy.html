Privacy and photo policy

What this website collects, and how the school handles photographs of children.

Information collected through this website

The only information this website collects is what you type into the admission enquiry form: your name, phone number, and optionally your child's name, the class you are asking about, your village, a preferred time to call, and your message.

It is used for one purpose — so the school office can call you back about admission. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside the school.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not track visitors between pages. The Google map on the home page and contact page is not loaded until you press the button to show it — if you never press it, nothing is requested from Google.

Photographs of students

Photographs of children are the part of a school website that most deserves care, so the rules here are set out plainly.

  • Consent is required for each child. No photograph in which a child can be identified is published without written permission from that child's family. Permission is recorded per child, not assumed for the whole class.
  • Names are kept apart from faces. A caption never pairs a child's photograph with their full name and class together.
  • Nothing that locates a child. Photographs are not published if they show bus route boards, timetables, identity cards, addresses or anything else that would tell a stranger where a particular child will be and when.
  • Location data is removed. Every photograph is re-processed before publication, which strips the location the camera recorded.
  • Consent can be withdrawn. A family may ask at any time for their child's photograph to be taken down, without giving a reason, and it will be removed.

Staff photographs and details

Teachers' names, qualifications and photographs are published only with each teacher's agreement, and are removed when a teacher leaves.
