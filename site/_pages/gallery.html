Our rules for photographs of children

  • No photograph of an identifiable child is published without written consent from the family. Consent is checked for each child, and is not assumed from a general form.
  • Captions never pair a child's photograph with their full name and class.
  • Nothing is published that shows where a particular child will be and when — bus route boards, timetables, identity cards or addresses in the background.
  • Location data is stripped from every photograph before publication, so that no image carries the coordinates of where children were standing.
  • A family may withdraw consent at any time and the photograph will be taken down. See the privacy and photo policy.
