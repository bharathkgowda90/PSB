What belongs here

  • Circulars — notices sent to parents, posted here as well so nothing is lost in a school bag
  • Holiday notices — unexpected closures and declared holidays
  • Events — what is coming up, and reports afterwards
  • Achievements — students and teachers who have done something worth noting
  • Admission announcements — when the admission window opens
