Snehanjali Rural Education Society (R.)

The registered society that founded and runs the school.

Purpose

Snehanjali Rural Education Society (R.) established Prashanth High School of Brilliance at K.M. Doddi in 2002. The society's purpose is rural education: bringing English medium schooling to families in this part of Maddur taluk who would otherwise have to look to a city for it.

The "(R.)" in the name indicates that the society is registered. Its registration details are published below so that parents and officials can verify them.
