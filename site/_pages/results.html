Names, photographs and scores of top scorers can be published here — but only with written permission from the student's family in each case. Permission for one year does not carry over to the next.

Students who need more support

A pass percentage does not tell you what happens to the students who find the examination hardest. That is often the more useful question for a parent whose child is not a natural examination taker.

A note on how these figures are published

Results will be published as they are, including years that were weaker. A school that shows a dip and a recovery is more believable than one that shows only its best year, and parents in this area talk to each other regardless.
