Getting here

The school is at K.M. Doddi, also called Bharathinagar, on the Maddur - Malavalli Road (SH-33) in Maddur taluk.

  • From Maddur town — take the Malavalli road south; K.M. Doddi is a short drive out of town.
  • From Bengaluru or Mysuru — leave the Bengaluru-Mysuru highway at Maddur and follow the Malavalli road.
  • By bus — buses on the Maddur to Malavalli route stop at K.M. Doddi.
  • By school bus — children travel on the school's own routes; see bus routes.
