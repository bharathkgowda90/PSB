School fees

What the fee covers, how it varies, and how to get the exact figure for your child's class.

Getting the exact amount

The fee is different for each class, and amounts are not published on this website. To get the figure for your child's class, telephone the office on 082322 98195, or send an enquiry and the school will call you back.

How the fee is structured

The fee rises with the class. Pre-primary classes cost less than the secondary classes, where there are more subjects, laboratory work and board examination preparation.

Fee bands by stage. Amounts are given by the school office on request.
Stage Classes Annual fee
Pre-Primary Pre-School, Pre-KG, LKG, UKG On request
Primary Class 1 to Class 5 On request
Middle Class 6 to Class 8 On request

Charged separately

These are normally additional to the school fee:

  • Transport — depends on the route; see bus routes
  • Uniform
  • Textbooks and notebooks
  • Board examination fee for Class 10, as fixed by the board

Worth reconsidering before launch

The school has asked not to show fee amounts. That is a legitimate choice, but in this market a parent who cannot find any figure often assumes the school is beyond their means and never calls. Publishing even a "from" figure or a band per stage would keep those families in the conversation. This is the school's decision to make — the page works either way.
