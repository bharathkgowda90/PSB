Admissions 2026-27

How to get a place at Prashanth High School of Brilliance, from the first enquiry to the first day.

When admissions open

Admissions for the coming year usually open after the annual examinations, in March and April. Enquiries are welcome at any time of year, and the office will tell you the current position.

Classes you can apply for

The school admits children into every class it teaches: Pre-School, Pre-KG, LKG, UKG and Class 1 through Class 10. Availability differs by class, and the entry points with most seats are usually Pre-School and Class 1.

Age on entry

Karnataka schools apply an age cut-off calculated on a fixed date. Bring your child's birth certificate to the office and they will tell you which class your child is eligible for.

The steps

  1. Enquire Send an enquiry through this website, telephone 082322 98195, or simply come to the office during working hours.
  2. Visit the school Come and see the classrooms, the laboratories and the playground, and meet a teacher. You do not need an appointment, though telephoning first means someone will be free to show you around.
  3. Collect and fill the application form The form is issued at the school office.
  4. Submit the form with documents Bring the documents listed below. Originals are seen and returned; copies are kept.
  5. Interaction For younger children this is an informal conversation to see how the child is placed, not a test. For higher classes the school may assess the level in main subjects so the child is placed correctly.
  6. Confirmation and fee payment Once a place is offered, the admission is confirmed on payment of the fee. The office will tell you the amount and the schedule.

Documents to bring

  • Birth certificate (original and one photocopy)
  • Transfer Certificate from the previous school, for admission into Class 1 and above
  • Previous school's report card or marks card
  • Passport size photographs of the child
  • Aadhaar card of the child and of a parent
  • Caste or income certificate, where a concession or reservation is being claimed
  • Study certificate, where applicable

Fees

The fee depends on the class. The office gives the exact amount for your child's class when you enquire — see the fees page for what the fee covers and how it is paid.

Common questions

My child does not speak English. Is that a problem?

No. Most children joining the younger classes arrive with little or no English. Teachers speak Kannada as well and use it when a child needs an explanation in the language they think in. Children pick up English quickly once they are settled.

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Mid-year admission is sometimes possible if a seat is free, for example when a family moves into the area. Telephone the office and ask.

We live in a village some distance away. Is there a bus?

School buses run to villages across the area. Check whether yours is covered on the transport page, or ask the office.

Do you take students into Class 9 or Class 10?

Admission into Class 9 and Class 10 depends on seats and on the student's previous record, since these are board examination years. Speak to the office directly.
