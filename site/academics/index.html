Academics

Karnataka State Board curriculum, taught in English, from a child's first year of school through to the SSLC examination in Class 10.

Curriculum and board

The school follows the Karnataka State Board syllabus and prepares Class 10 students for the Karnataka Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC) examination, conducted by the Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board.

The medium of instruction is English. Kannada is taught as a language subject throughout, and teachers use Kannada in class when a child needs an explanation in the language they think in. Children are not penalised for arriving with little English; that is a starting point, not a disqualification.

How children are assessed

Assessment follows the state board pattern: continuous assessment through the year alongside formal examinations. Parents are told how their child is doing at parent-teacher meetings, not only when the report card arrives.
