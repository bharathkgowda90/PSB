Mandatory public disclosure

Recognition, registration, infrastructure and staffing details, published so that parents and officials can verify them.

Why this page exists

Detailed public disclosure is compulsory for CBSE-affiliated schools. This school follows the Karnataka State Board, where the specific obligations differ and should be confirmed with the Deputy Director of Public Instruction rather than assumed from CBSE rules.

The page is published anyway. A parent choosing a school for a young child is entitled to check that it is properly recognised, and publishing this openly is a straightforward advantage over schools that publish nothing.
