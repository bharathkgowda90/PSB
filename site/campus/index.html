Campus and facilities

What is actually on the campus at K.M. Doddi, listed plainly so you know what to expect before you visit.

Numbers to be supplied

The sections below describe the facilities in general terms. Counts — how many classrooms, how many computers, how many library books — come from the school and will replace the gaps. Specific numbers are far more persuasive than descriptions, so these are worth collecting.

Classrooms

Separate classrooms for every class from Pre-School to Class 10, with the pre-primary rooms arranged for young children rather than as small versions of senior classrooms.

To be supplied

Number of classrooms, seating capacity per room, and whether any rooms have smart boards or projectors.

Laboratories

Science laboratory facilities for physics, chemistry and biology practical work, and a computer room used from the primary classes upward. Details of both are on the science and technology page.

Library

A library with reference books and reading books in English and Kannada, with timetabled library periods so that reading is part of the week rather than something optional.

To be supplied

Number of books, whether books can be borrowed and taken home, and library timings.

Playground and sports

An open playground for games and physical education, and space for the annual sports day. Sports offered are listed under student life.

To be supplied

Playground size, courts or pitches marked out, and equipment available.

Drinking water and toilets

Parents of young children ask about this more than about anything else on this page, and rightly so.

To be supplied

Drinking water arrangements and whether water is filtered or purified; number of toilets for girls and for boys; separate arrangements for pre-primary children; and how often facilities are cleaned.

Safety

A rural school with children arriving by bus has particular things to get right: a secure compound, a known procedure for who may collect a child, first aid on site, and a safe way to get an unwell or injured child to a doctor.

To be supplied

Compound wall and gate arrangements, whether there is CCTV, first aid facilities and trained staff, fire safety equipment, the nearest hospital or clinic, and the procedure when a child falls ill during the school day.

Assembly and events

Space for the daily assembly and for school events such as annual day, sports day and the science fair.
