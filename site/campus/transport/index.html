School bus routes

School buses bring children in from villages across Maddur taluk and the surrounding area, so that living outside K.M. Doddi is not a reason to miss out on a school place.

Sample data - not the real routes

The routes, villages and timings in the table below are examples only. They were made up to build and test this page, using village names from the area around the school. They are not the school's actual bus routes and must be replaced with the real route list before this site goes live.

Do not rely on anything in this table. To find out whether a bus comes to your village, telephone the office on 082322 98195.

Is my village covered?

If your village is not on the list, still ask. Routes are adjusted between years depending on where children are coming from, and a stop can sometimes be added.

Ask about your village Call 082322 98195

Transport charges

Transport is charged separately from the school fee and depends on the distance of the route. The office gives the amount for your route on request. See the fees page.

Bus safety

Putting a young child on a bus is a real act of trust, so it is fair to ask what stands behind it.

To be supplied

Driver licensing and verification, whether an attendant travels with each bus, seating capacity against the number of children carried, speed limits observed, GPS tracking if fitted, fitness certificates, and the procedure if a bus breaks down or is delayed.

About timings on this page

Only approximate route timings are published, never a stop-by-stop schedule tied to individual children. A precise public pickup list would tell a stranger exactly where a particular child will be standing and when.
