Student life

What happens between the lessons, and often what a child remembers years later.

The school day

The day starts with assembly, where the whole school gathers for prayer, announcements and occasionally a class presentation.

To be confirmed

School start and end times, assembly time, break and lunch timings, number of periods, and whether pre-primary follows a shorter day.

Sports and games

Physical education is timetabled for every class, and an annual sports day brings the whole school together.

To be supplied

Which sports are actually offered, whether the school takes part in taluk or district level competitions, and any achievements worth recording.

Events through the year

The events most schools in this area hold, subject to confirmation for this one:

  • Teachers' Day and Children's Day
