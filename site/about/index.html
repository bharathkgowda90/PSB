About our school

A Karnataka State Board school in Maddur taluk, teaching children from the surrounding villages in English medium since 2002.

Who we are

Prashanth High School of Brilliance was started in 2002 by Snehanjali Rural Education Society (R.) at K.M. Doddi, also known as Bharathinagar, on the Maddur - Malavalli Road in Mandya district.

The society was set up to bring English medium schooling within reach of families in this part of Maddur taluk, so that a child from a village here would not have to travel to a city for it. That is still what the school is for.

The school teaches every class from Pre-School through Class 10, and prepares students for the Karnataka SSLC board examination.

What "a school with science and technology" means

The phrase sits on the school crest, so it is worth being plain about what it does and does not mean.

It means the science laboratory and the computer room are part of ordinary school weeks rather than an occasional treat, and that children handle equipment themselves instead of watching a demonstration. It does not mean the school claims facilities it does not have. What is actually in the laboratories is listed, item by item, on the science and technology page.
