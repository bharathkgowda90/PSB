01 — What we do

The building blocks of a steady education.

Small classes, teachers who know every child by name, and a science and computer programme that children actually get their hands on.

Fundamentals first

Read well, write clearly, handle numbers without fear. Everything later rests on those three, so the primary years put them first and check that no child is quietly falling behind.

Science and technology

The phrase is on our crest, so we back it with specifics. From Class 6 students handle apparatus themselves and write up what they observe, rather than watching a demonstration from a desk.

Within reach

School buses run to villages across the area, so living outside K.M. Doddi is not a reason to miss out on a place. Ask the office whether a bus comes your way.
