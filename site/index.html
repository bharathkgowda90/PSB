An English medium school for the villages around K.M. Doddi

Prashanth High School of Brilliance has taught children from Pre-School to Class 10 in Maddur taluk since 2002. Karnataka State Board, English medium, run by Snehanjali Rural Education Society.

Admissions for 2026-27

Admissions usually open after the annual examinations, in March and April. Seats in each class are limited, so it helps to enquire early. You are welcome to visit the school and look around before you decide.

Why families choose this school

Science and technology

The school was built around science and technology teaching. Children use the science laboratory and the computer room as a normal part of their week, not as a once-a-term demonstration.

See the labs and equipment

English medium, local roots

Lessons are in English, taught by teachers who speak the children's home language too. A child who arrives with little English is helped, not left behind.

How each stage is taught

Buses to the villages

School buses run to villages across the area around K.M. Doddi and Maddur, so children from outside the town can attend without a daily struggle.
